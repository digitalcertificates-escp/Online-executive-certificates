How to apply

01 / Choose your certificate

Explore the subject, language, learning commitment, fees, and intake. Open the official programme page for the full curriculum and brochure request.

02 / Start your application

Select “Apply online” to visit ESCP’s official application portal. You may need to create an account or sign in. Follow the programme’s instructions and provide the requested information.

03 / Confirm your enrolment

ESCP reviews applications. Follow the admissions team’s guidance to confirm your place, funding arrangements, and access to the learning platform.
