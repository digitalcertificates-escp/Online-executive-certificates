Programme syllabus

Module 1 · Designing Sustainable Business Models
Course code: SUSTR-02Planned teaching: 12 h · Live sessions: 3 h
Florian Lüdeke-Freund

Design a programme with this module

Learning objectives

  • Analyse business models, their components, and value creation mechanisms in the context of companies and industries.
  • Evaluate the environmental, social, and economic implications of conventional and innovative business models in the context of sustainable development.
  • Apply key concepts, frameworks, and patterns of sustainable business model design in the context of real-world cases and business challenges.
  • Design sustainable business models by integrating sustainability principles, stakeholder perspectives, and pattern-based solutions in the context of organizational transformation and innovation.
Module 2 · Leadership for Sustainability Transformation
Course code: SUSTR-04Planned teaching: 12 h · Live sessions: 3 h
Carolin Waldner

Design a programme with this module

Learning objectives

  • Identify adaptive and complex sustainability challenges and select appropriate leadership approaches to approach them as leaders
  • Lead sustainability transformation processes by creating urgency, building coalitions, developing a clear vision, and sustaining long-term transformation
  • Apply systemic, reflective and holistic thinking to approach interconnected and contradictory sustainability issues
  • Understand and regulate your own emotions and effectively engage diverse stakeholders to drive sustainability transformation
  • Build resilience in the face of complexity and uncertainty while managing ambiguity and sustain engagement in long-term transformation processes
Module 3 · Risk Management
Course code: SUSTR-05Planned teaching: 12 h · Live sessions: 3 h
Michael Wiedmann
Ulrich Hagel

Design a programme with this module

Learning objectives

  • The participants should recognize the importance of sustainability risk-management.
  • The participants should be aware of the elements of an ESG risk-management system.
  • The participants should be able to implement the risk-management into the ESG-strategy.
